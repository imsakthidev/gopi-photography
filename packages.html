CLARITY & TRANSPARENCY

Investment FAQ

Common questions regarding pricing, payment terms, travel, and deliverable schedules.

How do we confirm a booking? +

After we discuss your date and coverage requirements, your date is formally blocked upon payment of a 40% advance booking deposit. The remaining balance is handled on the event day and upon final album approval.

Are there any hidden charges or travel fees? +

None. For events in Theni and surrounding taluks (Bodinayakanur, Cumbum, Periyakulam), travel is fully included. For distant districts across Tamil Nadu (Chennai, Coimbatore, Salem), modest travel and basic lodging are transparently quoted upfront.

Can we customize our package? +

Yes, 100%. Our packages (Essential, Signature, Cinematic) serve as transparent starting points. You can add extra sessions, additional photographers, same-day reels, or extra parent albums based on your exact celebration requirements.

How long does album and video delivery take? +

Within 7 to 10 days, you receive a preview selection of highlights for social sharing. The full color-graded digital gallery is delivered via Google Drive cloud link within 3 to 4 weeks. Once you select your favorite images, printed flush-mount albums are delivered within 4 to 6 weeks.
