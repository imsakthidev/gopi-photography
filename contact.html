QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you plan your photography and videography coverage with complete peace of mind.

Do you travel outside Theni? +

Yes, absolutely. While our primary studio is based in Theni, over 60% of our weddings take place across Madurai, Dindigul, Coimbatore, Pollachi, Tirupur, Salem, Trichy, Tirunelveli, and Chennai. We travel wherever your celebration takes us.

How far in advance should we book? +

Because auspicious Tamil Muhurtham dates (Subha Muhurtham days) are concentrated throughout the year, peak dates often book 3 to 6 months in advance. We recommend checking availability as soon as your wedding hall is confirmed.

What is your photography style? +

Our aesthetic is honest, documentary-driven, and natural. We capture authentic candid moments as they happen without forced posing, while ensuring respectful, graceful stage portraits of family elders. We never artificially bleach or whiten skin tones.

Do you provide both candid and traditional coverage? +

Yes. Every wedding requires both: dedicated candid photographers capturing the tears, secret glances, and joyous laughter, alongside experienced traditional photographers ensuring every ritual and elder is documented with clear lighting.

Do we receive all the raw unedited photos? +

Yes. In addition to your curated, master-edited high-resolution gallery, we provide the complete digital archive of captured photographs on high-speed USB pen drive or private cloud link for your family archives.

How long does photo and album delivery take? +

Within 7 to 10 days, you receive a preview selection of highlights for social sharing. The full color-graded digital gallery is delivered via Google Drive cloud link within 3 to 4 weeks. Once you select your favorite images, printed flush-mount albums are delivered within 4 to 6 weeks.

Can we customize our package? +

Yes, 100%. Our packages (Essential, Signature, Cinematic) serve as transparent starting points. You can add extra sessions, additional photographers, same-day reels, or extra albums based on your exact celebration requirements.

How do we confirm a booking? +

After we discuss your date and coverage requirements, your date is formally blocked upon payment of a standard 40% advance booking deposit. The remaining balance is handled around the event days.
