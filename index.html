QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you plan your photography and videography coverage with confidence.

Have a question not listed here?

Chat with Gopi directly →
Do you travel outside Theni? +

Yes, absolutely. While our home studio is in Theni, we regularly travel for weddings, receptions, and family celebrations across Madurai, Coimbatore, Dindigul, Tirunelveli, Chennai, and throughout Tamil Nadu and South India.

How far in advance should we book? +

Auspicious dates and peak Muhurtham season dates typically book 4 to 8 months in advance. Because we only accept one wedding story at a time to ensure personal artistic focus, we recommend getting in touch as soon as your date and venue are set.

Do you provide both candid and traditional photography? +

Yes. A South Indian wedding requires both: candid photographers who quietly move around capturing unscripted moments and raw emotions, and dedicated traditional photographers who ensure every family group, elder, and sacred ritual is thoroughly recorded.

Do you provide wedding videography and cinematic films? +

Yes. We offer both comprehensive traditional 4K videography (capturing the full ceremony from beginning to end) and cinematic highlight films edited like a movie trailer with custom music and real vows.

Do you provide drone coverage, and is it available everywhere? +

Yes, licensed aerial drone cinematography is available in our Cinematic package or as an add-on. Drone flight is strictly subject to venue rules, local police guidelines, and airport/airspace clearance.

How long does photo and album delivery take? +

Within 7 to 10 days, you receive a preview selection of highlights for social sharing. The full color-graded digital gallery is delivered via Google Drive cloud link within 3 to 4 weeks. Once you select your favorite images, printed flush-mount albums are delivered within 4 to 6 weeks.

Can we customize our package? +

Yes, 100%. Our packages (Essential, Signature, Cinematic) serve as transparent starting points. You can add extra sessions, additional photographers, same-day reels, or extra albums based on your exact celebration requirements.

How do we confirm a booking? +

After we discuss your date and coverage requirements, your date is formally blocked upon payment of a standard advance booking deposit. The remaining balance is handled around the event days.
